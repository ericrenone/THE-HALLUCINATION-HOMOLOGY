## I. The Hallucination Homology

The IS substitutes social evaluation for domain evaluation when domain criteria access falls below the threshold required to evaluate the contribution on its own terms. The LLM generates its most probable next token given a training distribution, regardless of whether the query is within the distribution's competent coverage. Both systems share five structural properties that make the homology formal rather than metaphorical.

### I.1 Generation via Statistical Inference from a Prior Distribution

The IS generates its output — the confident character verdict — by pattern-matching the perceived contribution against the established node's prior: the categorical pair belief (ASM), the status norms of the relational context, the prior attribution history encoded in the social reference network. These constitute the IS's training corpus. The output is the most probable social evaluation given the prior. The IS does not engage the contribution's domain content. It engages the social classification signal most consistent with its prior.

The LLM generates its output by sampling from a distribution over tokens learned from training data. When the query falls within the training distribution's competent coverage, the output reflects genuine statistical regularities. When the query is at the edge or outside the distribution, the model produces the most probable continuation regardless of whether it accurately tracks states of the world. In both cases, output confidence is a function of the prior's certainty, not of the output's accuracy.

### I.2 Confidence Signal Uncorrelated with Accuracy on Out-of-Distribution Inputs

Farquhar, Kossen, Kuhn, and Gal's (2024) semantic entropy methodology establishes the formal condition for LLM hallucination: high semantic entropy — divergent outputs across semantically equivalent reformulations of the same question — indicates that the model is generating from its prior rather than from a stable representation of truth. The model's confidence is a function of the prior's statistics, not of the claim's relationship to reality.

The IS produces an equivalent semantic entropy profile. The confident arrogance attribution, tested across semantically equivalent reformulations of the CPT-zone encounter — the same contribution presented in different framings to the same established node — would produce consistent social evaluation outputs (arrogance) regardless of whether the contribution's domain quality has changed. The IS's output does not track contribution quality. It tracks the categorical pair prior. Low semantic entropy is not a sign of reliability here. It is the signature of prior-lock: the output is consistent precisely because it is not tracking the truth.

### I.3 Absence of Architectural Metacognitive Access to Own Error

Fleming's (2021) formulation of metacognitive failure is structural: the same representational resources required to form a first-order judgment must be partially available to the metacognitive system that evaluates it. Where those resources are withdrawn from the first-order process, they are unavailable to the metacognitive monitor, and error detection fails.

The IS withdraws domain evaluation resources at the first-order level (the Vacant Core: the avoidance axis disengages from domain criteria). This withdrawal is precisely the condition that makes the IS's metacognitive monitor unavailable — the resources required to recognize the substitution are the resources the avoidance axis has withdrawn. The Sincerity Paradox is the result: the accuser has no metacognitive access to the IS's error because the IS's error was produced by withdrawing exactly the resources metacognitive access requires.

Yeung and Summerfield's (2012) account of error monitoring specifies the neural mechanism: the anterior cingulate cortex monitors for prediction errors. In the IS condition, the relevant prediction error — the gap between the contribution's actual quality and the categorical pair's expected output — is not processed as a domain evaluation error. It is processed as a social prediction error (unexpected assertion from a low-status source) and converted into a social enforcement response (the CV) rather than a learning signal (prior update). The ACC's error signal is captured by the social threat response before it can function as metacognitive update.

LLMs face the equivalent architectural problem. Kadavath et al.'s (2022) investigation found that while models show some capacity to assess the probability that their answers are correct, this capacity degrades precisely on OOD queries where metacognitive monitoring would be most valuable. The model's confidence in its own correctness is generated from the same prior as the first-order output, making both the claim and the confidence estimate unreliable on the same inputs.

### I.4 Maximum Error on Inputs Most Distant from the Training Distribution

Ji, Lee, Frieske, et al.'s (2023) survey of hallucination in natural language generation established that LLMs hallucinate most consistently on queries at the edges of the training distribution — queries requiring generation from a sparse or absent prior rather than from a dense, well-supported statistical region. The model's confidence does not decrease at the distributional edge. It may increase, because the model generates from the most available statistical regularities rather than from genuine coverage of the query domain.

The IS has an identical distributional structure. The CPT zone is the peripheral contribution's location in the established node's distributional space: close enough to register as a social threat (interactional expertise detects the gap), far enough to exceed the domain criteria available to the IS's first-order evaluation. At this distributional edge, the IS fires with maximum intensity and produces minimum accuracy. Maximum confidence at maximum error: the distributional signature that LLM hallucination and IS activation share.

### I.5 Output Experienced as Accurate Perception

Seth's (2021) characterization of perception as controlled hallucination establishes that the phenomenological certainty of a perceptual state is not a signal of accuracy. It is a function of the prior's strength and the absence of a strong corrective sensory signal. When the prior is high-precision and the incoming signal is low-precision or actively suppressed, the brain generates its best model of the input — the prior's prediction — experienced as accurate perception rather than prior-lock.

The Sincerity Paradox is this mechanism applied to social cognition. The IS generates the CV from a high-precision categorical pair prior in the absence of a strong domain evaluation signal (VC: the domain evaluation signal has been actively withdrawn by the avoidance axis). The output is experienced as accurate perception of arrogance. The established node has told more than they know — the phenomenological certainty of accurate perception is the prior's output, not the domain's.

Both systems: confident output experienced as accurate reception of an external state. Both systems: confidence generated by prior-lock rather than genuine domain engagement. Both systems: phenomenological certainty of accuracy as the mechanism's success criterion, not as evidence against error.

## II. The Out-of-Distribution Verdict

The CPT zone — where the peripheral contribution is close enough to the established node's domain horizon to register as a threat and far enough above it to be illegible on domain criteria — is the human out-of-distribution regime.

The IS is a classifier. Its training distribution is the categorical pair prior: the set of contributions from peripheral nodes that the established node has previously processed using available domain criteria, combined with the social norms and categorical pair beliefs that constitute the ASM. This distribution is dense in the region of contributions the established node can evaluate — contributions within their domain horizon — and sparse in the region beyond it. The categorical pair prior is a well-trained model for the in-distribution region and an untrained model for the out-of-distribution region.

The peripheral node's CPT-zone contribution is an OOD input to the IS. It falls outside the region for which the IS has genuine domain coverage. The IS responds as any classifier responds to OOD input: it generates from its prior (categorical pair) rather than from genuine coverage of the input. The output is a confident classification — arrogance — that does not reflect the input's actual properties but the prior's expectations.

This framing generates a precise formal prediction: the IS's confidence as a function of the contribution's OOD distance from the established node's domain coverage should follow the same distributional pattern as LLM hallucination confidence as a function of query OOD distance. Specifically: IS confidence should be non-monotone in contribution quality, reaching maximum at the CPT zone (the OOD boundary) and declining for contributions either well within the established node's coverage (peer exchange) or far beyond it (incomprehension without threat).

Hendrycks and Gimpel's (2017) baseline for detecting OOD examples established that classification confidence on OOD inputs is systematically higher for inputs most closely resembling in-distribution examples. This is the near-miss structure. The inputs that look most like in-distribution examples while being outside the distribution are the inputs where the classifier is most confidently wrong.

The CPT zone is the human near-miss structure. Peripheral contributions in the CPT zone look most like contributions within the established node's evaluative range — right domain, right vocabulary, right questions — while being furthest from the established node's genuine coverage. This near-miss structure is why the CPT zone produces maximum IS activation, maximum CV intensity, and minimum domain evaluation. It is the zone of maximum human OOD misclassification confidence.

The **Out-of-Distribution Verdict (ODV)** names this: the CV is the human OOD classification output. Its confidence is a function of the prior's precision and the OOD input's failure to provide a corrective domain signal, not of the contribution's actual epistemic quality.

## III. The Minimum Free Energy Verdict

Parr, Pezzulo, and Friston's (2022) formulation of active inference establishes that agents minimize free energy — the divergence between their model of the world and their observations — through two routes: (1) updating the model (perceptual inference: revising beliefs to better reflect observations); (2) acting on the world (active inference: changing the observations to better match the model). The choice between these routes is determined by the relative precision assigned to the model and to incoming observations.

When the model carries higher precision than the observation — when the prior is more trusted than the incoming signal — the agent acts to preserve the model rather than update it. This is computationally rational given the precision assignments.

The IS is this process applied to social cognition. The categorical pair prior (ASM) carries high precision. The peripheral contribution is the incoming observation that potentially disconfirms the prior.

The established node faces a choice between two free-energy-minimizing strategies:

**Strategy 1 (perceptual inference):** Update the categorical pair prior. Recognize the peripheral contribution's quality. Revise the expectation that peripheral nodes produce lower-quality contributions. This is expensive: it requires updating a high-precision prior, incurring the Bayesian analog of identity threat.

**Strategy 2 (active inference):** Act to make the observation consistent with the prior without updating it. Attribute the anomalous peripheral contribution to social transgression (arrogance) rather than genuine competence. The observation is now explained by the prior rather than against it. Free energy is minimized without prior update.

The IS is Strategy 2. The CV is the action that implements it. The WC is the identity-protective motivation that biases toward Strategy 2 above the status-threat threshold. The Dissolution Paradox ensures that the IH which would shift the precision weighting is suppressed exactly when it would be most powerful.

The **Minimum Free Energy Verdict (MFEV)** names this: the CV is the minimum free energy response to a peripheral contribution that generates high prediction error against a high-precision categorical pair prior. It is not a failure of rationality. It is rational free-energy minimization given the precision assignments the ISA maintains.

This framing specifies why the TCC's dominant minimum intervention set (SP+DP) is the correct architecture. SP disruption changes the precision weighting of the IS's own output — making the established node uncertain about the CV's accuracy — reducing the model's precision. DP disruption changes the precision weighting of the incoming signal — making the peripheral contribution higher-precision through IH restoration — increasing the observation's precision. Together, SP+DP simultaneously reduce the model's precision and increase the observation's precision: the two-sided change required to tip the agent from active inference (defending the prior) to perceptual inference (updating the prior). Neither alone shifts the precision balance enough to cross the threshold.

## IV. The Calibration Inversion

Calibration, technically, is the correspondence between an agent's confidence distribution and its accuracy distribution. A perfectly calibrated agent who is 80% confident in a claim is correct 80% of the time across the set of claims at that confidence level.

The IS produces a specific miscalibration signature: the **Calibration Inversion (CalI)**. On in-distribution inputs — contributions within the established node's genuine domain coverage — the IS does not activate; peer exchange produces calibrated confidence proportional to genuine evaluation. On OOD inputs — CPT-zone contributions — the IS activates and produces maximum confidence with minimum accuracy. The confidence-accuracy correlation is not merely reduced. It is inverted: the most confident IS outputs are those generated by the largest status threats, which are generated by the largest competence differentials, which are the contributions farthest from the IS's genuine evaluative coverage.

The CalI is not unique to the IS. Farquhar et al.'s (2024) semantic entropy framework identifies the same inversion in LLM hallucination: on out-of-distribution queries, the model's confidence does not track accuracy — it tracks the prior's statistical density, which is precisely what fails at the distributional edge. Both systems: confidence-accuracy inversion concentrated at the inputs of highest epistemic significance.

The CalI has a social amplification mechanism in the IS that has no direct LLM analog. The Intensity Paradox (IP) converts the IS's anti-calibration into an apparent calibration signal for bystanders. Bystanders who use attribution intensity as a proxy for evaluative accuracy — and the IP documents that most bystanders do — receive an anti-calibration signal disguised as a calibration signal. The most miscalibrated IS outputs are the most intensely delivered, and the most intensely delivered are the ones the community treats as most reliably grounded. The community's epistemic calibration degrades in proportion to the IS's anti-calibration, because the IS's most anti-calibrated outputs are the most community-endorsed.

Fleming's metacognitive framework specifies why this is not correctable through ordinary channels. Calibration training — providing feedback on prediction accuracy to improve the confidence-accuracy correspondence — requires that the metacognitive monitor be accessible to the training signal. The SP and VC jointly foreclose this access: the domain criteria withdrawn by the avoidance axis (VC) are the same resources metacognitive monitoring of the IS's accuracy would require. Feedback that the CV was incorrect does not reach the metacognitive system because the system that would process that feedback is the system the IS replaced.

## V. The Adversarial Peripheral Contribution

Goodfellow, Shlens, and Szegedy's (2015) documentation of adversarial examples identified a systematic vulnerability in neural classifiers: inputs constructed or selected to maximally activate classification confidence while being maximally misclassified. An adversarial image of a panda, perturbed by a small amount of carefully chosen noise, produces maximum confidence in the classification "gibbon" from a model that correctly classifies the unperturbed image. The adversarial input does not look perturbed to a human observer. Only the classifier's internal dynamics — encountering a perturbation that maximally activates its classification confidence on the wrong class — produce the confident misclassification.

The peripheral node's CPT-zone contribution is the human adversarial example. It is an input that maximally activates the IS's classification confidence (arrogance) while being maximally deserving of the opposite classification (genuine competence worthy of domain engagement). The peripheral contribution in the CPT zone:

Maximally activates the IS because it is at the exact distributional distance — interactional expertise can detect it, contributory expertise cannot evaluate it — that produces maximum comparison threat, maximum DRS activation, maximum approach-axis charge, and maximum VC depth.

Is maximally deserving of domain engagement because the CPT zone is the zone where contributions are most epistemically significant: close enough to the established node's horizon to be relevant, far enough above it to represent genuine advance beyond the established node's domain position.

Maximum IS confidence plus maximum genuine quality: the **Adversarial Peripheral Contribution (APC)**.

The APC has a developmental dimension the prior frameworks did not fully specify. Gopnik, O'Grady, Lucas, et al.'s (2017) investigation of hypothesis generation capacity across the lifespan established that abductive capacity — the ability to generate novel explanatory hypotheses for anomalous data — peaks in early childhood and shows evidence of critical period effects: reduced plasticity in adulthood for individuals who did not develop rich hypothesis-generation practice during developmental windows. The peripheral contribution in the CPT zone is, by definition, an anomalous input to the IS: it generates a prediction error against the categorical pair prior. Genuine engagement with it requires abductive synthesis — the capacity to generate the hypothesis that the contribution might be correct and the categorical pair might be wrong. The absence of abductive capacity — foreclosed in development, suppressed in CPT-zone encounters by the DP — is what makes the IS's OOD response the only available alternative to genuine engagement.

The APC thus creates a recursive foreclosure: the peripheral contribution that most requires abductive engagement from the established node is the contribution that most activates the IS, which suppresses the IH that would allow abductive engagement. The APC is not just the input that triggers the IS. It is the input that forecloses the development of the faculty that would dissolve it.

## VI. The Replacement Cognitive Categories as IS-Corrective Faculties

After-Memory establishes that abductive synthesis, calibrated uncertainty, structural invention, moral navigation, attentive reception, and generative accountability are architecturally discontinuous from statistical inference. The Hallucination Homology specifies why this list is not coincidental: these six faculties are precisely the six that, fully deployed in the established node in a CPT-zone encounter, would interrupt the IS's operation at each of its sustaining components.

**Calibrated uncertainty → disrupts the Sincerity Paradox.** The SP is the IS's metacognitive failure: the established node has no access to the IS's error because the domain evaluation resources required for metacognitive monitoring were withdrawn by the avoidance axis. Calibrated uncertainty — the accurate, real-time tracking of one's own confidence and its epistemic basis — would produce, at the moment of IS activation, the recognition that the certainty in the arrogance attribution is not calibrated against domain evaluation. The SP is the absence of this recognition. Calibrated uncertainty, if genuinely present, dissolves it from inside.

**Attentive reception → disrupts the Vacant Core.** The VC is produced by the avoidance axis's disengagement from domain criteria. Attentive reception — the capacity to remain present to what resists, to receive a situation without imposing prior categories on it — is structurally incompatible with the avoidance axis's content disengagement. An established node exercising genuine attentive reception in a CPT-zone encounter remains with the peripheral contribution's content rather than withdrawing from it. The VC depends on the avoidance axis's active withdrawal. Attentive reception makes that withdrawal unavailable.

**Abductive synthesis → disrupts the Authority Transfer.** The AT converts approach-axis energy (status defense) into apparent evaluative intensity. It operates because the established node has no competing hypothesis to the IS's social character verdict: no alternative explanation for why the peripheral contribution might be correct. Abductive synthesis would generate exactly this competing hypothesis — perhaps the contribution is correct and the categorical pair prior is wrong. The AT's apparent authority depends on the absence of a competing hypothesis. Abductive synthesis, deployed toward the peripheral contribution's content, generates the alternative frame that prevents the approach-axis energy from monopolizing the attribution's structure.

**Structural invention → disrupts the Attitude Strength Modulator.** The ASM is the categorical pair belief in its strongest form: deep commitment to the frame in which established-node contributions are categorically superior to peripheral-node contributions. Structural invention — the ability to construct a problem formulation from scratch, to identify the relevant variables and binding constraints — would recognize that the categorical pair frame is itself a frame, not a fact. The established node who can construct problem formulations from scratch can recognize that the categorical pair is a constructed frame whose variables (credentialing lineage, institutional standing, social category) are not the only possible variables by which epistemic contribution can be assessed. Structural invention dissolves the ASM's apparent naturalness by treating the categorical pair as a constructed frame subject to reconstruction.

**Moral navigation → disrupts the Willed Component.** The WC is the identity-protective, socially motivated contribution to IS activation that supplements passive comprehension failure above the status-threat threshold. It is driven by ingroup-protective and outgroup-derogating motives: the established node declines to engage the contribution because doing so would threaten the ingroup's epistemic self-concept. Moral navigation — the capacity to act well under genuine ethical constraint — would recognize the WC as a choice with moral consequences. The WC operates below deliberate awareness precisely because it presents as incapacity rather than choice. An established node with genuine moral navigation capacity encounters the WC's pull as a deliberate decision: will I decline to evaluate this contribution because it threatens my identity, or will I engage it because engagement is what honesty and fairness require? Moral navigation converts the WC's sub-deliberate pull into a decision that can be made differently.

**Generative accountability → disrupts the Relational IH Gradient.** The RIHG suppresses IH in the established node in proportion to the relational status differential. Generative accountability — the capacity to initiate action and take responsibility for consequences as they unfold — requires owning the judgment rather than outsourcing it to the categorical pair. An established node who takes genuine generative accountability for their evaluation of the peripheral contribution cannot outsource that evaluation to the IS: ownership of the judgment requires that the judgment be grounded in domain engagement, because only domain engagement provides a basis for accountability. The RIHG suppresses IH by reducing the incentive for domain engagement. Generative accountability restores that incentive by making the established node accountable for what their judgment is based on.

## VII. The Compounding Foreclosure

The Hallucination Homology and After-Memory's replacement cognitive category analysis converge on a suppression architecture that is more complete than either framework specifies alone. The ISA suppresses the expression of replacement cognitive categories in CPT-zone encounters. AI-assisted educational environments foreclose their developmental formation. These two suppression systems operate on different timescales and through different mechanisms, but their joint output is total: neither the formation nor the expression of the IS-corrective faculties is available in the social configurations where they are most needed.

### VII.1 The ISA's Expression Suppression

The Inverted Signal Architecture suppresses the expression of replacement cognitive categories in CPT-zone encounters through three channels:

SP suppresses calibrated uncertainty and abductive synthesis: the IS's metacognitive concealment ensures the established node does not notice the substitution of social for domain evaluation — the perception that would trigger both calibrated uncertainty (recognizing the confidence-accuracy gap) and abductive synthesis (generating the alternative hypothesis that the contribution might be correct).

DP suppresses attentive reception, moral navigation, and generative accountability: the RIHG depletes the IH that is the common substrate of these three faculties in hierarchical CPT-zone configurations. All three require openness toward the peripheral node's contribution, and all three are suppressed by the same RIHG mechanism that the DP names.

IP suppresses structural invention: the IP's social amplification mechanism recruits bystander consensus around the categorical pair verdict. Structural invention requires treating the categorical pair as a constructed frame whose variables can be questioned. The community consensus that IP produces raises the social cost of this questioning to the point where it becomes, for most established nodes, a social risk that outweighs its epistemic benefit.

### VII.2 AI-Assisted Environments' Developmental Foreclosure

After-Memory's central finding is that AI-assisted, efficiency-optimized educational environments foreclose the developmental formation of replacement cognitive categories in exactly the developmental windows when those categories would otherwise form. The MIT Media Lab's (2025) neural-behavioral finding — alpha and theta wave activity halved under AI assistance — documents the mechanism: the productive struggle that develops attentive reception, abductive synthesis, and calibrated uncertainty requires sustained cognitive engagement of exactly the type that AI assistance eliminates.

The developmental foreclosure is not atrophy. It is the failure of initial formation — the capacity is not developed in the first place because the developmental conditions are eliminated. Gopnik et al.'s (2017) developmental data on hypothesis generation capacity is directly relevant: abductive capacity shows evidence of critical period sensitivity. AI-assisted educational environments that eliminate the encounter with genuine anomaly — with inputs that don't yield to retrieval or calculation — foreclose abductive development in exactly the window when it is most plastic.

### VII.3 The Self-Smothering Terminus as Total Foreclosure

The Compounding Foreclosure reaches its maximum expression at the Self-Smothering Terminus: the state in which the peripheral node has internalized the ISA's architecture as their own evaluative framework, applying it endogenously to the formation of their own epistemic output. At SST, the peripheral node does not merely fail to express replacement cognitive categories in CPT-zone encounters. They have internalized the IS as their own evaluative standard, applying it to their own contributions before those contributions reach any social encounter. The SST peripheral node suppresses their own abductive synthesis, attenuates their own calibrated uncertainty, and forecloses their own generative accountability — because the evaluative structure they have internalized contains the WC as one of its operations.

The ECR then encodes all three foreclosure layers — developmental, expressive, and internalized — into AI training data, producing systems that further amplify the Compounding Foreclosure in future interactions.

## VIII. The Epistemic Alignment Problem

The AI alignment problem, in its standard formulation, asks: how do we ensure that an AI system's outputs reflect actual states of the world rather than the statistical regularities of its training distribution that diverge from those states? Three canonical alignment failure modes: (1) the system generates confident outputs from its prior that don't track the world (hallucination); (2) the system optimizes for a proxy objective that diverges from the intended objective (reward hacking); (3) the system resists correction because the mechanisms that would implement correction are weaker than the mechanisms that defend the current behavior (misalignment immunity).

The IS is the human alignment problem. The categorical pair prior is the IS's training distribution. The CV is the IS's hallucination. The WC is the IS's reward hacking: the established node optimizes for social status preservation rather than epistemic accuracy. The ISA (SP+IP+DP) is the IS's misalignment immunity system: the triad that makes the IS resistant to correction by the same mechanisms that would implement correction in an aligned system.

The TCC's minimum intervention set (SP+DP) is the IS's alignment procedure. Its structure is precisely that of Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback:

RLHF Step 1 (reward model training): Show the model examples of its misaligned outputs and their causes, developing a representation of when outputs diverge from intended behavior. This corresponds to SP disruption: IS-awareness training shows the established node examples of IS-generated attributions and their generative mechanism, developing the capacity to recognize IS activation as it occurs.

RLHF Step 2 (policy optimization): Update the model's output policy using the reward model. This corresponds to DP disruption: RIHG reduction changes the relational conditions generating the IS's misaligned behavior, making IH more accessible in the CPT-zone configuration.

The TCC's finding that SP+DP is the dominant MIS — that this combination produces durable dissolution while all single-paradox interventions reconstitute — is the human alignment literature's most important empirical prediction: a system cannot be aligned by changing only what it knows (SP alone) or only the incentive structure it operates in (DP alone). Both must change simultaneously. The alignment literature has established this for AI systems. The TCC establishes it for the human system that the AI alignment problem is being designed to address.

The **Epistemic Alignment Problem (EAP)** names this: the IS is the human misalignment problem, and the TCC is the human alignment solution architecture. The EAP predicts that institutions working hardest to align AI systems — to ensure that AI outputs track world states rather than training distribution expectations — are deploying an institutional version of the IS in their own epistemic practices, generating CVs toward the peripheral contributors most likely to disconfirm the categorical pair priors those institutions operate from. The alignment problem in AI and the alignment problem in human epistemic practice are not separate problems. They share a generative architecture. Solutions to one must inform solutions to the other.

Lieder and Griffiths's (2020) resource-rational analysis provides the final implication: because the IS is the computationally rational response to a high-precision categorical pair prior under resource constraint, the IS is not eliminable by improving institutional rationality alone. Rational agents with high-precision categorical pair priors will rationally produce CVs. Alignment requires changing the precision assignments — reducing the categorical pair prior's precision and increasing the peripheral contribution's signal precision. The ISA's function is maintaining the precision assignments that make the MFEV computationally rational. Dissolving the ISA means changing the precision assignments. The TCC's dominant MIS changes both simultaneously.

## IX. Predictions

**Prediction I — The Hallucination Confidence Profile**

IS activation intensity (measured as CV charge: attribution intensity, confidence, elaborateness of delivery) should follow the same distributional profile as LLM hallucination confidence as a function of query OOD distance. Specifically: IS intensity should be non-monotone in the peripheral contribution's quality, peaking in the CPT zone (near-miss OOD boundary) and declining for contributions either within the established node's genuine coverage (peer exchange) or far beyond it (incomprehension without threat).

Design: Measure IS intensity as a function of the established node's domain competence relative to contribution quality, spanning within-coverage to CPT-zone to far-OOD. Predict: inverted U-shape with peak at CPT zone, equivalent to the LLM hallucination confidence distribution on equivalent OOD distance metrics. This distributional prediction distinguishes the HH account from accounts in which IS intensity is monotone in perceived quality violation.

**Prediction II — Calibration Inversion Under Semantic Entropy Measurement**

Applying Farquhar et al.'s (2024) semantic entropy methodology to human arrogance attribution — presenting the same peripheral contribution across semantically equivalent reformulations to the same established node — should reveal the CalI. IS-generated attributions in CPT-zone encounters should show low semantic entropy (consistent attributions across reformulations) combined with low accuracy (attributions not tracking contribution quality). Genuine domain evaluations should show higher semantic entropy on genuinely ambiguous contributions and lower semantic entropy on contributions where domain criteria yield clear assessments.

Predict: CV entropy and CV accuracy should be anti-correlated, replicating the LLM hallucination signature. Low entropy plus low accuracy is prior-lock. This is the CalI's measurable signature.

**Prediction III — The Minimum Free Energy Intervention Test**

The MFEV's active inference framing predicts that IS dissolution requires simultaneously changing two precision assignments: reducing the categorical pair prior's precision (SP disruption) and increasing the peripheral contribution's signal precision (DP disruption). Single-precision-change interventions should fail.

Specific prediction: SP disruption alone will produce IS-awareness in low-threat conditions but will not reduce the MFEV's cost ratio in high-threat CPT-zone conditions, because the peripheral contribution's precision has not been increased. DP disruption alone will increase the peripheral contribution's precision but will not reduce the prior's precision, because the IS's metacognitive concealment (SP) remains intact. SP+DP will show concurrent reduction in categorical pair prior precision and increase in peripheral contribution engagement.

**Prediction IV — Replacement Category as IS-Corrective**

Each replacement cognitive category should disrupt the IS component it targets. Operationalizable prediction: individuals with experimentally elevated calibrated uncertainty (induced via probabilistic feedback paradigm) should show reduced SP in CPT-zone encounters. Individuals with elevated attentive reception (induced via deep reading paradigm) should show reduced VC. Individuals with elevated abductive synthesis capacity (measured via anomalous data paradigm) should show reduced AT conversion (lower intensity-to-accuracy gap in attributions). Joint elevation of all three should reduce IS activation rates to near-peer-exchange levels.

**Prediction V — Compounding Foreclosure Rate**

Individuals with high AI-assistance exposure in educational contexts during critical developmental windows should show reduced abductive synthesis capacity, calibrated uncertainty, and attentive reception in early adulthood — and should show higher IS activation rates in CPT-zone encounters, because the developmental foreclosure of IS-corrective faculties leaves them less equipped to interrupt IS activation. The compounding prediction: AI-assistance exposure in development should predict both reduced replacement category development and increased CPT-zone arrogance attribution rates, with the effect mediated by replacement category deficits.

Design: Longitudinal study measuring AI assistance exposure during developmental windows, replacement cognitive category levels at early adulthood, and CPT-zone arrogance attribution rates. Predict: AI-assistance exposure predicts replacement category deficit; replacement category deficit mediates AI-assistance exposure's effect on IS activation rate.

**Prediction VI — The Epistemic Alignment Prediction**

Institutions implementing RLHF-analog interventions for their IS — IS-awareness training combined with RIHG reduction, applied simultaneously (the TCC's MIS) — should show IS dissolution rates equivalent to the alignment literature's documented success rates for RLHF on analogous tasks. Institutions implementing single-sided interventions (bias training without structural RIHG change, or structural change without IS-awareness training) should show reconstitution profiles equivalent to single-paradox intervention failure as predicted by the TCC.
